- `mark.svg` and `favicon.svg` are byte-for-byte copies of the CyberNative brand kit v1.1.1 mark.
- Source: public-safe company brand kit, version v1.1.1.
- Licenses: SIL OFL 1.1 texts are bundled in `public/LICENSES/`.

Both are self-hosted; no runtime font request is made.
